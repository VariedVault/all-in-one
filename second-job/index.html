Class VI applies no allowances, so its withholding rarely matches your final tax. Your annual Steuererklärung reconciles the difference: a refund if too much was withheld, or a top-up if too little. In practice a refund often comes from deductible expenses (Werbungskosten) that this simplified estimate does not model. Social insurance is not refunded.

Estimate only. The actual refund depends on your full personal tax situation, deductible expenses (Werbungskosten), and other factors not modelled here.
