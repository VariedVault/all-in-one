Simplified approximation, not the official Lohnsteuer PAP formula. It uses the same piecewise-linear §32a tax approximation as the pension calculator, applies the Grundfreibetrag per tax class (doubled for III, none for V/VI) and a Vorsorgepauschale approximated by your social insurance contributions. It does not account for the Kinderfreibetrag in the tax calculation itself, private health insurance (PKV), or other individual circumstances. Social insurance uses the 2026 employee rates and ceilings. For planning direction, not payroll or filing accuracy.
