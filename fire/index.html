A simplified projection in today's euros, with no inflation applied to expenses or the target. Each investment category compounds at the constant annual return you give it (real markets are volatile), and your existing net worth grows at the average of those returns. It assumes your monthly amounts and expenses stay flat, and that the withdrawal rate you choose is sustainable. The 4% rule is a rule of thumb, not a guarantee. For direction, not a plan.
